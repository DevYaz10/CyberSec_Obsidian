>[!quote] Definition
> **WhatWeb** is ==an open-source, Ruby-based web scanner used for technology fingerprinting and reconnaissance on websites==. It uses over 1,800 plugins to detect content management systems (CMS), web servers, JavaScript libraries, frameworks, version numbers, email addresses, and HTTP headers.

- **Technology Fingerprinting:** Identifies web servers (such as Apache or Nginx), CMS platforms (like WordPress or Joomla), analytics tools, and backend frameworks.

- **Aggression Levels:** Supports scan modes ranging from stealthy/passive (Level 1) to aggressive (Level 3) and heavy (Level 4) depending on how thoroughly you want to probe a target. 

- **Flexible Outputs & Tuning:** Exports results in brief, verbose, JSON, XML, SQL, MongoDB, or Elasticsearch formats, with options for custom headers, proxies (including TOR), threads, and timeouts.

- **Reconnaissance Focus:** Focuses on identifying technologies rather than exploiting vulnerabilities.
